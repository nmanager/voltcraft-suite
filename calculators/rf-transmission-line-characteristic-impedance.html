Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the standard impedance?

Most RF systems use 50Ω as the standard impedance for cables and PCB traces.

Q: Does substrate thickness matter?

Yes, the height (h) between the trace and the ground plane is a critical factor in determining impedance.
