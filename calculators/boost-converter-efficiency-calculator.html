Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is my boost converter getting hot?

High heat is usually caused by excessive conduction losses (high RDS(on)) or switching losses due to high gate capacitance at high frequencies.

Q: How can I improve efficiency?

Use a MOSFET with lower RDS(on), select an inductor with lower DCR, and ensure your switching frequency is optimized for your inductor size.
