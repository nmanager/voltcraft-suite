Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why use a Grounded Coplanar Waveguide?

GCPW provides better isolation and lower radiation loss compared to standard microstrip lines in high-frequency applications.

Q: What is the importance of the gap width?

The gap width (between the signal trace and the ground plane) is a primary variable in controlling the characteristic impedance of the transmission line.
