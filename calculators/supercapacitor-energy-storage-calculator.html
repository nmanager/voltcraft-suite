Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can supercapacitors replace batteries?

They are ideal for short-term high-current bursts, but their high self-discharge rate makes them unsuitable for long-term energy storage.

Q: What is the primary benefit of using these?

They provide high power density, allowing small batteries or energy harvesters to support high-current radio transmissions.
