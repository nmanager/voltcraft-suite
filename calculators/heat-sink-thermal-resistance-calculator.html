Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is Rthcs included in thermal calculations?

Rthcs (Case-to-Sink thermal resistance) accounts for the thermal interface material (TIM) or grease between the component and the heat sink, which is critical for accurate calculations.

Q: What happens if the calculated thermal resistance is negative?

A negative result indicates that the power dissipation is too high for the component to operate safely at the given ambient temperature, even without a heat sink.
