Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is output ripple important?

High voltage ripple can cause instability in precision ADCs and introduce noise into sensitive wireless communication modules.

Q: Does ESR affect this calculation?

Yes, actual ripple is the sum of capacitive ripple and ESR-induced ripple (ΔI * ESR). This calculator focuses on the capacitive component.
