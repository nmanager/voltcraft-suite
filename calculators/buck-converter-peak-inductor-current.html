Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is peak current important?

Exceeding the saturation current of an inductor leads to a drop in inductance, causing excessive ripple and potential switch failure.

Q: What is a typical ripple ratio?

A standard industry practice is to set the ripple current to 30% of the maximum output current.
