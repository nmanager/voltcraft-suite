Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many days of autonomy should I plan for?

For mission-critical CCTV, 3 to 5 days is standard to account for consecutive cloudy days.

Q: Does the system voltage affect battery capacity?

Yes, higher system voltages reduce the total Amp-hour requirement for the same amount of Watt-hours.
