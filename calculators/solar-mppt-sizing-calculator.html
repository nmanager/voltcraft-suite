Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why include temperature in the calculation?

Solar panels produce higher voltage in colder temperatures. If you ignore this, you risk exceeding the MPPT input voltage and damaging the controller.

Q: What happens if I exceed the MPPT voltage?

Exceeding the maximum input voltage typically results in immediate failure or permanent damage to the charge controller's input stage.
