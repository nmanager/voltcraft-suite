Formula & Theory

K = 10^{\frac{dB}{20}}, R_{shunt} = Z_0 \frac{K+1}{K-1}, R_{series} = \frac{Z_0}{2} \frac{K^2-1}{K}

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why use a Pi-network?

The Pi-network is symmetric and provides excellent impedance matching for bidirectional signal transmission.

Q: What is the K factor?

K is the voltage ratio, calculated as 10 to the power of (dB/20), used to relate attenuation to resistance values.
