Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is the factor 10 included?

The factor of 10 is a common engineering safety margin to ensure the ripple voltage on the capacitor remains low during the switching period.

Q: What happens if the capacitor is too small?

The high-side driver may trigger an undervoltage lockout (UVLO), causing the MOSFET to turn off prematurely or not at all.
