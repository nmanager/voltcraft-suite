Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is attenuation important?

Excessive attenuation reduces the effective range of antennas and can cause signal-to-noise ratio issues.

Q: Does cable quality matter?

Yes, dielectric material and shielding density significantly impact the attenuation constant provided by manufacturers.
