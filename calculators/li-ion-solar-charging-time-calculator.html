Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is efficiency less than 1?

Charging losses occur due to MPPT converter inefficiency, cable resistance, and heat dissipation.

Q: Does this account for peak sun hours?

This tool calculates total active charging time; you must divide by your local peak sun hours to get real-world calendar days.
