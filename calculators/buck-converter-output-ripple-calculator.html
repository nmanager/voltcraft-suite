Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is ESR important for ripple?

The Equivalent Series Resistance (ESR) of your output capacitor directly multiplies the inductor ripple current, often being the dominant factor in voltage ripple.

Q: What is an acceptable ripple for IoT?

For most 3.3V microcontrollers, a ripple under 50mV is considered safe to prevent logic errors or sensor inaccuracies.
