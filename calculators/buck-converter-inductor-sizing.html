Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is inductor ripple current important?

Keeping ripple current between 20% and 40% of the maximum output current balances efficiency, physical component size, and output voltage ripple.

Q: What happens if the inductor value is too low?

A low inductance causes higher peak currents, which can lead to inductor saturation, increased switching losses, and higher output voltage noise.
